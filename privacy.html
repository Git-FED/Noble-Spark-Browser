Site information

Privacy notes.

These pages are static files intended for GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. This bundle does not include an application server or its own payment processing.

Local page behavior

The site’s age confirmation is an advisory self-attestation saved in this browser tab’s session storage for the current session. It is not identity verification or legal-age verification. No analytics, tracking pixels, or first-party account system are included in this file bundle.

Static hosting providers may process ordinary server logs when serving pages. Consult the hosting provider’s privacy information for its practices.

Third-party services

When you choose a provider, that service receives the information needed for its page or checkout and applies its own terms and privacy practices. This can include PayPal, Stripe, GitHub Sponsors, Buy Me a Coffee, NOWPayments, Discord, and Substack. On payment pages, provider widgets are loaded only after the advisory age confirmation is accepted. The Discord widget and newsletter page connect to their providers when you open those pages.

Do not send payment details or passwords to the project by email or chat. Payment credentials, provider configuration, eligibility, and compliance are managed by the relevant payment provider.

Newsletter

The newsletter form sends the email address you enter to GitFed’s Substack endpoint. If the embedded request is unavailable, the page opens Substack’s own subscribe page. Substack handles subscription records and confirmation emails.

Questions

No contact email was supplied in this bundle, so this page does not invent one. For account, payment, data, or receipt questions, use the support route provided by the relevant third-party provider.
