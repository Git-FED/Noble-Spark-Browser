Terms and scope

Use within permission.

This project is an example browser-testing skeleton. It is not a full browser, proxy-rotation service, security bypass, or authorization grant.

You are responsible for obtaining written authorization, staying within the exact approved scope, following the target site’s terms, robots guidance, rate limits, API rules, and applicable law.

Project limits

  • No CAPTCHA solving or security-check bypass.
  • No evasion of access controls, authentication, bot protections, rate limits, or account security.
  • No fingerprint impersonation, credential stuffing, private-data scraping, proxy scraping, or IP-ban evasion.
  • Persistent cookies and local storage remain in a local ignored profile for an authorized test account; never commit or share that profile.
  • Stop on access denial, repeated rate limits, explicit opt-out, or when the owner asks.

Third-party providers

Links to payment, community, newsletter, or hosting services leave this static site and are governed by those providers’ own terms and policies. Payment and billing are not processed by the local HTML files.

This page is project guidance and is not legal advice. Review responsible-use guidance and privacy notes.
